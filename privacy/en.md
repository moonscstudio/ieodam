# Ieodam Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Ieodam ("the app") is an e-book reader for books you keep on your own NAS, Google Drive, or device (TXT, EPUB, comic ZIP/CBZ, PDF). It lets you continue reading where you left off on another device. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and how it is used.

## 1. Summary

- The developer **does not run any server and does not collect or receive your personal information.**
- Everything the app handles is stored only **on your device** and in **storage you connect yourself** (your NAS or your Google Drive).
- The app has no ads, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting tools.

## 2. Information the app handles and where it is stored

| Information | Purpose | Stored in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Book files | Opening and showing your books | Your device, or the NAS / Google Drive you choose |
| Reading position, bookmarks, time read, device name (e.g. "iPad") | Continuing on another device | Your device, and the app folder on the NAS or Google Drive you choose |
| Display settings (font, text size, page direction, etc.) | Keeping your reading view | Your device |
| NAS address, username, password | Connecting to your NAS | Your device (the password is kept in the iOS Keychain / Android encrypted storage) |
| Google account email address | Showing which account is connected | Your device |

None of this is sent to the developer. The app connects only to the NAS address you enter and, if you connect Google Drive, to Google's servers.

## 3. Google Drive

- Connecting Google Drive is optional and starts only when you tap "Sign in with Google".
- The app requests only the **`drive.file`** Google Drive scope. With it, the app can access **only files it created or that you opened with it**. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.
- The app creates an `Ieodam` folder in your Drive and keeps your books, reading positions, and bookmarks there.
- [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) also applies to Google sign-in and Google Drive.
- Ieodam's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses this information only for reading and syncing your reading position. It is never used for advertising, shared with others, or sold.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

The developer collects no personal information, so nothing is shared with or processed by third parties on the developer's behalf. Information saved to your NAS or Google Drive stays in your own storage.

## 5. Retention and deletion

- Information on your device is deleted when you delete the app. You can also remove connections with "Sign out" or in the storage settings.
- Books and reading-position files on your NAS or Google Drive can be deleted directly in that storage (for example, the `Ieodam` folder in Google Drive).
- You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time under [Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

## 6. Permissions

- **Internet**: to connect to your NAS and Google Drive.
- **Local network (iOS)**: to connect to a NAS on your home Wi-Fi.
- **File sharing (iOS)**: to let you add books with Finder or iTunes.

## 7. Security

- Your NAS password is kept in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android encrypted storage).
- Google Drive is accessed over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The NAS connection uses HTTP or HTTPS depending on the address you set for your NAS.

## 8. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

## 9. Your rights

Because the developer holds none of your personal information, you can view, correct, or delete it directly on your device and in your own storage, as described in section 5. If you have questions, please contact us below.
